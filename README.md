# My Favorite Series

## Breaking Bad

*Breaking Bad* is one of my favorite television series. It tells the story of Walter White, a chemistry teacher who becomes involved in the illegal drug business after being diagnosed with cancer.

### Why I Like It
- Interesting storyline
- Great acting and characters
- Unexpected plot twists
- Exciting episodes

### My Favorite Character
*Jesse Pinkman* – I like his character because of his development throughout the series.

"One of the best crime drama series I have watched."
